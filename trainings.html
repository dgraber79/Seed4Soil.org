Trainings

Word Engagement Discipleship Training using oral inductive Bible study methodology for Scripture storytelling and dialogue sessions.

1
STRATEGIES 1 - Training

Come to God's Word the way Jesus and His disciples did — out loud, together, in questions and dialogue. You will learn the five strategies, tell your first story, and leave with a way of engaging Scripture that needs no books, no buildings, and no expert in the room. Typical format: 2–3 day intensive.

STRATEGIES 2 - Training

Practitioner Level 2 — practice and develop your Bible storytelling skill. Five participant-led storytelling & dialogue sessions through the 7 Steps, with coaching at every turn. Typical format: two-day intensive.

Leadership STRATEGIES

Cultivating the Leader's Soil — leadership strategies from God's Word, from hidden foundations (daily bread, hearing & doing, humility, the way of the learner) to developing your own vision, mission, and goals.

4
STRATEGIES 1 for Oral Cultures - Training

Most of the world learns by listening, not by reading. Seven strategies for bringing God's Word to oral learning communities — through story, dialogue, song, and shared life. Reading is not required to participate fully.

STRATEGIES 2 for Oral Cultures - Training

For storytellers already telling Bible stories in their communities. The 8 Steps of Obedience — a complete discipleship foundation for new believers, taught entirely through story, memory verse, and dialogue. Typical format: four-day intensive.

Custom Word Engagement Events

Not every group needs a standard training format. Seed4Soil offers custom workshops and Word engagement events shaped around the specific needs of a congregation, ministry team, cross-cultural workers, or oral learning community. We begin not with a predetermined curriculum but with a question: What does this group most need in order to hear, receive, and share God's Word? Contact us to explore what a custom event might look like for your context.
