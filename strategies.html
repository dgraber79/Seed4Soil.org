Key Seed4Soil Training and Methodology Strategies

Bible Engagement Strategies

Five strategies for effective Word engagement — modeled after how Jesus himself taught.

What are key Seed4Soil training and methodology strategies for effective Word engagement? These five strategies are drawn from Jesus' own teaching style and methods — using stories, examples, and questions rather than lectures, creating space for people to hear and respond personally.

1

Plant

Foundational Strategy

We are soil to receive the seed of God's Word! God's Word functions as seed containing the mystery of reproducing life. Make God's Word your Words! Plant God's Word, not men's word! Make God's Word your Sermon!

2

Hear

Good Soil Strategy

Good soil "hears the word, accepts it, and bears fruit." The good soil "comes to Jesus, hears His Word, and does it." The parable begins and ends with a command: "HEAR!"

3

Discover

Good Sowing Strategy

"Jesus taught using stories, examples, and questions most of the time in His teaching as opposed to lecturing. He created space for people to hear and choose whether or not to respond." He was "a master of storytelling and dialogue."

4

Space

Good Seed & Sowing Strategy

God's thoughts and communication transcend human understanding. Even Jesus communicated exclusively through His Father's Word, received directly from intimate communion. "According to Jesus, if you eat God's Word in your body you will live forever!"

5

Trust

Good Soil & Sowing Strategy

Jesus "created space for people to hear and choose whether or not to respond." Dialogue questions enable discovery-based learning instead of passive reception. They respect listener dignity and foster ownership of ideas, promoting curiosity and openness to God's Word.
