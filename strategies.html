The Five Strategies

Five practices.
One living method.

Five strategies of Jesus — embodied in seven steps of Scripture storytelling and dialogue — pointing toward one word.

PLANT + HEAR + DISCOVER + SPACE + TRUST = Abiding

"Abide in Me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in Me." — John 15:4

These are not five separate lessons taught in sequence and then left behind. They are five conditions — five acts of a single living practice that Jesus first modeled at twelve years old in the Temple and embodied throughout His ministry. Together, practiced in the seven steps of Scripture storytelling and dialogue, they equal one word: abiding.

1

PLANT

Plant seed in soil

We are soil — formed from dust, made to receive. The seed is God's living Word. When we plant what He said and step aside, the mystery of the Kingdom begins.

HEAR

Hear & obey God's Word

Being heard is so close to being loved that they are indistinguishable. Hearing God's Word truly and deeply is how faith comes — and faith that takes root obeys.

Explore Strategy 2 → 3

DISCOVER

Use stories, examples & questions

Jesus asked 339 questions and directly answered about 8. Truth discovered is truth owned. The finder is the owner — and the owner is the one who acts.

Explore Strategy 3 → 4

SPACE

Build sacred space to hear & obey

Silence builds the walls. Listening builds the floor. Questions open the doors. Obedience is the foundation. Without space, the Word cannot be heard — and what is not heard cannot be obeyed.

Explore Strategy 4 → 5

TRUST

Use God's Word as spirit & life

The Spirit and the Word are one breath. Conviction and transformation belong to the Spirit — not to us. Plant the seed. Build the space. Step back. Trust.
