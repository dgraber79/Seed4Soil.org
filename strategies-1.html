Course Overview

STRATEGIES 1 is a Basic Level 1 Bible Storytelling and Word Engagement Training. The course centers on the primary question: "How can we come to Jesus, hear His Words, and do them?" (Luke 6:46–49)

Course Structure

  • Six Bible storytelling and dialogue sessions
  • Four group activities for storytelling practice
  • Five teaching sessions covering key strategies
  • Technical and methodological sessions

Typical format: 2-3 day intensive (flexible scheduling available)

Five Core Strategies Covered

  1. Plant — Foundational Strategy
  2. Hear — Good Soil Strategy
  3. Discover — Good Sowing Strategy
  4. Space — Good Soil & Sowing Strategy
  5. Trust — Good Seed Strategy
