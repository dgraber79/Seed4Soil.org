The Soil Drinks

Active, Sustained Reception

Picture dry ground when rain falls. The soil does not argue with the rain — it simply receives, slowly and completely, until what was dust becomes life-bearing. This is what hearing God's Word looks like. It is not passive. It is the active, sustained opening of yourself to what God is saying — until what He says takes root.

Hearing Produces Faith

The Word Itself Plants Faith

Much of what passes for Bible teaching does not give people the chance to hear God's Word directly — the teacher's voice fills the room, Scripture becomes a proof text, and the listener leaves with someone else's ideas ringing in their ears, not the living Word itself. But faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the Word of God — not by hearing opinions or commentary. The Word itself, heard directly, is what plants faith. Without faith it is impossible to please God.

Obedience

Obedience Is Hearing Completed

"Why do you call Me 'Lord, Lord,' and not do the things which I say?" Luke 6:46 · NKJV
"If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word, and My Father will love him, and We will come to him and make Our home with him." John 14:23 · NKJV

Hearing that does not lead to obedience is not really hearing at all. You cannot obey what you have not received. And what you have truly received, you will obey.

In This Training

What You Will Discover

In this training, you will practice receiving God's Word as good soil — giving it space to land, taking root in your memory, and bearing on your life. You will also learn to create that same space for the people you teach: giving them room to hear, not filling it with your own voice.

The soil that hears and obeys is the soil that bears fruit — fruit of attachment to the Vine.
