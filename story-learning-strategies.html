7 Steps — Learning the Story

  1. Choose the story appropriate to discipleship stage and listeners' biblical understanding
  2. Learn story structure by dividing into memorable parts with clear beginning, middle, end
  3. Visualize the setting mentally to identify with and experience the narrative
  4. Imagine the action occurring — crowds, movements, emotions, character interactions
  5. "Use your voice to project the images you've imagined" with appropriate volume and mood
  6. Memorize accurately; avoid adding non-biblical details since oral learners retell exactly
  7. Practice repeatedly until delivery becomes natural, matching cultural context and style

Quick Method to Learn the Story by Memory

  1. Read aloud or listen while following with imagination and body movement
  2. Close Bible and retell everything remembered in order, twice without looking back
    • Don't require word-for-word accuracy initially as it hinders deep learning
    • After learning naturally, refine using Bible's exact language with accessible translation
  3. Read aloud or listen again with imagination and movement
  4. Close Bible and retell remembered content in order, twice through
  5. Repeat until the story becomes natural and alive
