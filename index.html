What is Seed4Soil?

Plant Seed in Soil

Seed4Soil exists to see people encounter the living Word of God and be changed by it. We train disciples and disciplemakers in oral, story-based Bible engagement — accessible methods rooted in how Jesus himself taught — so that communities can hear Scripture, experience it together, and respond.

Key Questions

Understanding the Parable

"The sower sows the Word." Seed is "the Living Word of God — the Word of God became flesh and dwelt among us." Soil represents the human heart: "And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground." Humans are made in God's image from soil, and serve as the receptacle for the Seed of God's Word.
"To you it has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God; but to those who are outside, all things come in parables." Those "coming close, hearing, and responding" can understand.
How can the Living Word of God grow in a body made of soil and bear fruit? How can the Word become flesh in my soil? How can a human life become the place where a good God lives and works? All this happens and more, and this is the mystery that our Lord wants to reveal to us. Will you come and see?
The parable begins and ends with a command: "HEAR!" Good soil "hears the word, accepts it, and bears fruit." The good soil "comes to Jesus, hears His Word, and does it." Our training programs are designed to help individuals and communities cultivate this kind of receptive heart.
