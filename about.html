What We Are

A Bible engagement and training ministry

Seed4Soil is a Bible engagement and discipleship training ministry. We exist to help followers of Jesus plant God's Written Word deeply — in their own hearts, and in the hearts of those they disciple, train, and lead.

We work with disciples, church leaders, trainers, and missionaries across a wide range of cultures and contexts — from established congregations to oral communities in the Global South. Our methods are simple enough to be used by anyone, yet deep enough to transform communities where God's Word is planted and tended over time.

Our Mission

Plant God's Word. Trust His Spirit. Bear fruit that remains.

The mission of Seed4Soil is drawn directly from Jesus' teaching in the Parable of the Sower. Jesus was not describing a general principle about communication — He was describing the Kingdom of God: what happens when God's living Word enters a prepared heart, takes root, and multiplies.

Our mission has three inseparable parts:

  1. Plant God's Word — not men's words. God's Written Word is the seed. It carries life within itself. Our role as sowers is not to improve the seed or substitute our own ideas for it, but to plant it faithfully and create the conditions in which it can take root.
  2. Trust His Spirit. We are not the ones who make the seed grow. The Holy Spirit illuminates, convicts, and transforms — through the very Word we plant. Our task is to get out of the way and give Him room.
  3. Bear fruit that remains. The goal is not a moment of decision but a life of abiding — disciples who hear God's Word, are changed by it, and multiply it in others. This is the good soil: 30, 60, 100 fold.
The Name

Why Seed4Soil?

The name comes from the Parable of the Sower. Jesus describes four kinds of soil — four conditions of the human heart — and the different ways the same seed is received. The seed is God's Word. The soil is us.

Seed4Soil captures both the call and the question: the seed is ready — is the soil? And if not, what does it take for soil to become good? This is the question the 5 Strategies are designed to help answer — for the disciple, the leader, and the community.

Our Approach

Oral. Inductive. Spirit-dependent.

Our trainings and materials are built on three convictions:

  1. Oral engagement. Most of God's Word is narrative — story. Jesus taught through stories, questions, and dialogue. The early church transmitted the gospel orally for decades before anything was written down. We recover this method not as a curiosity but as a primary vehicle for the Spirit's work.
  2. Inductive discovery. Truth you discover for yourself is truth you own. And truth you own is truth you obey. Rather than delivering conclusions, we use story and question to lead participants into their own encounter with God's Word — so they emerge as owners, not recipients.
  3. Spirit-dependence. We plant and water. God gives the growth. Every training, every facilitated session, every story told is an act of trust — that the Spirit will do what He promised: lead His people into all truth.
What We Do

Training disciples and equipping trainers

Seed4Soil offers in-person and resource-based training in two primary streams:

  1. The 5 Strategies. A foundational framework for Bible engagement drawn from the Parable of the Sower — Plant, Hear, Discover, Space, Trust. These strategies are taught sequentially and practiced in real group settings.
  2. The 7 Steps. A practical oral inductive method for learning, retelling, and facilitating biblical stories — applicable in any cultural or literacy context, from urban churches to oral communities in the field.

Trainings range from introductory workshops to deeper equipping for leaders and trainers who multiply the method in their own communities and networks.

The Word Became Flesh

Everything Seed4Soil does is an expression of this: the Word does not remain abstract. It becomes flesh — it enters real hearts, real communities, real soil. When God's Word is planted in a prepared heart and tended by His Spirit, it grows. It multiplies. It becomes the life of a disciple, the health of a church, the transformation of a community.
